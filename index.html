    Network & security

    Your machine, reachable only by you.

    The Mac mini is not placed on the public internet. Access runs through a path we control, and every connection is authenticated before it reaches the machine.

    01

    Your device

    Your own laptop, on any network — office, home, hotel or mobile hotspot.

    →
    02

    Outbound tunnel

    The connection is opened from inside, not the outside. No inbound port ever listens on the internet.

    →
    03

    Identity check

    Every request is authenticated against your identity before it is allowed through.

    →
    04

    Your Mac mini

    One physical machine, allocated to you alone and reachable only through that path.

    ⚿

    No inbound ports

    There is no open port on the public internet for an attacker to find, scan or connect to. Nothing is listening from the outside.

    ◎

    Access follows identity

    Access is granted to a verified identity rather than to a shared password, with a second factor required by default.

    ⇄

    Encrypted in transit

    Traffic between your device and the machine uses modern TLS. No plaintext protocol is exposed to the internet.

    ▤

    Separate per customer

    Each customer gets their own machine and their own account. No shared home directory and no shared credentials.

    ⌘

    Key-only login

    Password authentication is disabled on the host. Sign-in requires a key that you hold, not a password that can be guessed.

    ◷

    Access is recorded

    Authentication events and access decisions are logged, so unusual activity can be identified and reviewed.

    What we never do

    • Expose the machine on a public IP address
    • Ask you to open a port forward on your router
    • Hand out a shared account used by several customers
    • Enable password login or store your password

    The exact access method is confirmed during onboarding and depends on your network and the agreed configuration.
